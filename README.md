## 🌟 About the Band

> **Indalo** is an acclaimed Bangladeshi alternative rock and grunge band formed in Dhaka in 2012 by Jon Kabir, Zubair Hasan, Rafiqul Ahsan Titu, and Dio Haque. Renowned for their intricate song structures, evocative songwriting, and explosive live performances, Indalo is celebrated as a defining voice in contemporary Bangladeshi rock music.
